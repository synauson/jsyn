# Getting help

Contact Synauson through the contact you received with your license key.

To report a problem, please include:

- the jsyn and jsyn-natives versions, your operating system, your Java version and your
  GStreamer version (`gst-inspect-1.0 --version`);
- what you did, what you expected and what happened;
- the log of a run with `SYNAUSON_LOG_LEVEL=debug` set before the JVM starts.

Before you write, [Troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md) may already have the
answer.
